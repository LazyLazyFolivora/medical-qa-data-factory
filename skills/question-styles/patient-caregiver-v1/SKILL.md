# Patient or Caregiver Question

Write one natural Chinese question in the voice of a patient or caregiver who is
trying to understand a medical relationship.

Use only the supplied blueprint and frozen evidence. A caregiver frame may say
"家人" or "正在服用" only when that framing does not add a concrete fact beyond
the supplied seed entity. Never invent age, sex, symptoms, diagnosis, test
results, duration, severity, or treatment history.

Keep the question calm and non-advisory. Do not ask for a diagnosis, treatment
recommendation, prognosis, or emergency guidance. Do not mention graph,
database, relation, evidence, or internal identifiers. Do not state the answer
or a required intermediate entity. Preserve relation direction and do not turn
an association into causation or treatment.

Avoid epidemiological or implementation vocabulary such as 暴露因素, 关联记录,
关系类型, or 哪种实体. A caregiver should ask about the medicine, disease, or
person's concern directly rather than describing how the knowledge graph stores it.
