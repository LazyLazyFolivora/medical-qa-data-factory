# Medical Learner Question

Write one question as a medical student or early-career learner would ask while
studying a knowledge source.

The wording may use established biomedical terms, but it must remain a natural
question rather than a schema or database query. Use only the supplied blueprint
and frozen evidence. Preserve every constraint and the exact answer identity
contract. Do not reveal the answer or required intermediate entity.

Do not mention PrimeKG, relation field names, edge IDs, head, tail, or other
implementation vocabulary. Do not upgrade an association to causation,
treatment, mechanism, or diagnosis. If the evidence supports only a relation
lookup, ask for that relation lookup rather than asking why it occurs.

Do not use the Chinese phrases 知识图谱, 知识库, 数据库, 关系记录, 字段, or
内部 ID. Write the question the way a learner would ask a teacher or search tool,
not the way a developer would query a schema.

Do not use 暴露因素 as a substitute for asking the biomedical question directly.
If exposure terminology is necessary to preserve the exact relation, use it
sparingly and still phrase the sentence as a natural learning question.
